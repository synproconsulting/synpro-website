Most organisations know when a facilities contract isn't working. What's harder is establishing
*why* — and whether the problem is the supplier, the specification, the governance around it, or
the expectations set when it was signed.

I assess the service you receive against what you pay for and what comparable organisations
achieve: the supplier's actual capability rather than their capability statement, cost and
structure benchmarking, and the gap between contracted and delivered service.

Sometimes that leads to a re-tender. Often it doesn't — a well-run remediation with the incumbent
is cheaper and faster than a market process, and I'll tell you when that's the better answer.
