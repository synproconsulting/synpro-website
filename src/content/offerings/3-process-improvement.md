A good contract with a capable supplier still underperforms if the operation around it is weak. If
nobody owns the relationship, if the SLAs measure things that don't matter, or if governance
happens only when something goes wrong, value leaks out regardless of what was signed.

My background is industrial and mechanical engineering, and that's the lens: look at how work
actually flows, find where effort is wasted or accountability is unclear, and design something
better.
