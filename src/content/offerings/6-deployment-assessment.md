You already own the system. Adoption is poor, the data can't be trusted, or the value case that
justified it hasn't materialised.

This is assessment work on a platform rather than a supplier: how it's actually used versus how it
was designed, what state the data is in, and where process and system have drifted apart. It's
independent of whoever implemented it — which is usually the point.

Data is often the real answer. Profiling, standards, mapping and cleansing rules, and load
readiness are hands-on work I do directly, and they're what unstick most stalled deployments.
