Implementations need a team. I'm not one, and I won't pretend otherwise.

Where a client or an implementation partner needs senior functional capability on a delivery team,
I join it. That means business analysis, functional and solution design, testing and UAT, training,
and the data work — profiling, mapping, cleansing, migration, and reconciliation — which is
consistently where these programmes stall.

I've done this repeatedly across IWMS and CMMS platforms, in both on-premise and SaaS deployments,
in energy, financial services, manufacturing, and government environments.
