You've bought the platform and engaged an implementation partner. You're now spending heavily on a
programme you have limited ability to evaluate. The design decisions sound reasonable. The status
reports are green until they aren't. And the people best placed to tell you whether it's going well
are the people being paid to deliver it.

I sit on your side of that. As your technical authority in programme governance, I review and
challenge design decisions, hold deliverables to a standard before you sign them off, and hold the
partner to what they contracted to deliver. Not adversarially — most partners do good work — but
because a client with no independent technical voice in the room is at a structural disadvantage.

This is an extension of what I already do on sourcing transitions, where representing the client
while a new supplier mobilises is a core part of the engagement. Same role, different supplier,
higher technical content.
