Sourcing facilities services well is harder than it looks. The market is crowded, offerings are
hard to compare, and the difference between a good contract and an expensive one usually comes down
to how the process was structured before anyone submitted a price.

I run that process end to end. More than 25 years sourcing cleaning, food services, and maintenance
contracts for large corporate and industrial organisations has taught me the method matters more
than the template: understand what you actually buy today, design an approach that fits your
operation, take it to a market you may not know well, and hold the commercial and technical
evaluation to a standard that survives internal challenge.
